## 2. Why Job Seekers Win Decisively as the Primary Audience

1. **Exact Feature Alignment in Code:**
   * `{driveLink}` resume PDF attachments with visual cards (`[PDF] Resume.pdf`).
   * Company and hiring manager name guessing (`{company}`, `{name}`).
   * Split-span email detector specifically built for LinkedIn hiring posts and Naukri listings.
   * `📡 Scan Feed` auto-scroll designed for LinkedIn feed job posts (*"We're hiring! Drop resumes to..."*).
   * Lead application tracking (`Pending` vs `Submitted`).
2. **Competitor Blind Spot:**
   * Giants like Hunter.io, Snov.io, Apollo, and ZoomInfo are hyper-focused on enterprise B2B sales teams charging $49–$299/month.
   * Job seekers cannot afford $99/mo subscriptions and get locked out by free-trial credit walls.
   * QuickMail Finder requires **no sign-up, zero credits limit, 100% on-device privacy, and is completely free**.
3. **High Organic Search Volume with High Urgency:**
   * Millions of job seekers search Google and Chrome Web Store daily for: *"how to find recruiter email on linkedin"*, *"hiring manager email finder"*, *"email finder for job applications"*.
4. **Community Virality:**
   * Job seekers share job hunt hacks and tools enthusiastically on LinkedIn, Reddit (`r/jobs`, `r/recruitinghell`, `r/cscareerquestions`), and Telegram/WhatsApp career groups.

## 3. Official Positioning Architecture

### Primary Audience
* **Active Job Seekers, Career Changers, and Recent Graduates** seeking direct contact with hiring managers, talent acquisition teams, and HR leads on LinkedIn, job boards, and company websites.

### Secondary Audience
* **Freelancers, Agency Founders, and Independent Consultants** doing targeted client outreach and cold pitches.

---

### Primary Use Case
* **Direct Hiring Manager & Recruiter Cold Outreach:**
  * Spot hiring manager emails on LinkedIn posts, company career pages, and profiles.
  * Click **1-Click Draft** to generate a pre-filled, personalized application email with a Google Drive resume link attached.
  * Track submitted applications in local history.

### Secondary Use Cases
1. **Feed Sourcing:** Auto-scrolling LinkedIn feeds with `📡 Scan Feed` to extract all recruiter emails from "We're hiring" posts into an Excel export.
2. **Freelance Lead Generation:** Finding founder/marketing emails on agency and SaaS websites for cold pitch proposals.
3. **Bulk Outreach Campaigning:** Sending personalized 1-on-1 outreach to multiple leads without stacking duplicate tabs.

---

### Core Product Promise
> **"Find any hiring manager or recruiter email on any webpage, and launch a personalized Gmail or Outlook draft in 1 click — 100% free, no sign-up, zero data tracking."**

## 4. Key Value Pillars for Store Copy & Marketing

1. **Skip the Job Board Black Hole:** Reach real decision-makers directly instead of submitting to automated ATS portals where resumes get lost.
2. **1-Click Personalized Drafts:** Automatically inserts the recruiter's name, company name, custom message, and resume link.
3. **True Privacy & Zero Cost:** No monthly credit caps, no account registration required, zero data sent to external servers.
4. **Outreach History Tracker:** Know exactly who you emailed, which company they belong to, and when you submitted.
